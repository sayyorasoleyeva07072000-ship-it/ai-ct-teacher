AI-CT Teacher app

AI-CT Teacher is an interactive learning app for future English teachers. Choose a classroom task, then work through the AI-CT 7C Cycle: Context, Consult, Critique, Check, Challenge, Conclude and Reflect. You think first, consult artificial intelligence, examine and verify its response, challenge it, and write your own professional conclusion.

The app includes eight English-teaching tasks, an evidence-checking table, a transparent six-component critical-thinking rubric, progress tracking, reflection history and a teacher dashboard with demo data.
