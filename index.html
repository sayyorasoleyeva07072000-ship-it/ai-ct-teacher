AI-CT Teacher

AI-CT Teacher is an educational platform for developing the critical thinking skills of future English teachers through structured interaction with artificial intelligence. It supports English teacher education, teacher training and AI-assisted learning: students solve real classroom problems, ask an AI for advice, then analyse, verify and challenge its answer before they write their own decision.

The AI-CT 6C Cycle has exactly six stages: Context, Consult, Critique, Check, Challenge and Conclude (the last one includes a short reflection). The platform includes 32 tasks in 8 categories, a Method Challenge quiz, a Team Competition game for AI in English language teaching and English language education, student accounts and, with the optional secure server, teacher classes.
